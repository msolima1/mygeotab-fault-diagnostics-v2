For SSO users: Get your session from an existing MyGeotab login.

Get this from browser DevTools on alpha.geotab.com (see instructions below)

How to get your Session ID:
  1. Log into alpha.geotab.com/g560
  2. Open DevTools (F12 or Cmd+Option+I)
  3. Go to Console tab
  4. Type: JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem('geotabAPI_credentials')).sessionId
  5. Copy the result and paste above
